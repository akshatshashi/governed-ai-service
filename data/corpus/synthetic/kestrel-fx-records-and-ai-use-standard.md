# Kestrel FX: Record Keeping, Privacy and AI Use Standard (synthetic)

Provenance: a synthetic internal standard written for this project. Kestrel FX is a fictional Australian remittance and foreign-exchange business. This document does not describe any real organisation.

Standard owner: Head of Operations Risk. Version 1.4.

## Record retention

Transaction records and customer identification records must be kept for 7 years. For customer identification records, the 7 years runs from the end of the customer relationship. Records must be retrievable within 5 business days if AUSTRAC asks for them.

Records of alert reviews, escalations, unusual activity reports and approval decisions are kept for 7 years as well, so that every compliance decision can be reconstructed.

## Handling personal information

Collect only the personal information needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Do not record full card numbers, Tax File Numbers or passwords in case notes or free-text fields. Mask identifiers in reports where the full value is not needed.

## Use of AI assistants

Staff may use only approved AI assistants, and only as decision support. Every output from an AI assistant must be reviewed by a person before it is used, and the person who acts on the output remains accountable for the decision.

Do not enter customer personal information into an unapproved AI tool. The approved compliance assistant redacts personal information automatically, keeps an audit record of every question, and cannot take a high-impact action, such as flagging a customer, without approval from a second person.

## Access control

Access to customer records is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter. Shared accounts are prohibited.
