# Kestrel FX: Transaction Monitoring and Escalation Policy (synthetic)

Provenance: a synthetic internal policy written for this project. Kestrel FX is a fictional Australian remittance and foreign-exchange business. This document does not describe any real organisation.

Policy owner: AML/CTF Compliance Officer. Version 3.2.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Kestrel FX branches and to online remittance and foreign-exchange channels.

## Alert generation

The transaction monitoring system generates an alert when, for example:

- a customer makes two or more cash transactions between AUD 9,000 and AUD 9,999 within 5 business days (possible structuring);
- a customer's cash transactions across branches total AUD 10,000 or more in one business day;
- a remittance is sent to a jurisdiction identified as high risk; or
- a customer's activity changes sharply from their established profile.

## Alert review

Level 1 (L1) analysts must review every transaction monitoring alert within 2 business days of it being generated. The L1 analyst either closes the alert with a written rationale, or escalates it to Level 2.

Level 2 (L2) investigators must complete their investigation of an escalated alert within 5 business days. If the investigator forms a suspicion, they must escalate to the AML/CTF Compliance Officer immediately, and no later than the end of the same business day.

The AML/CTF Compliance Officer decides whether a suspicious matter report is required and ensures it is lodged within the legal deadline of 3 business days, or 24 hours for terrorism financing.

## Four-eyes principle

Any decision to flag a customer or transaction for compliance review, to exit a customer, or to close an alert on a high-risk customer as a false positive must be approved by a second authorised person. The person who raised a flag cannot approve it.

## Unusual activity reports

Any employee who notices unusual activity must raise an internal unusual activity report (UAR) within 1 business day. Employees must not tell the customer that a UAR or SMR has been raised, or is being considered.

## Sanctions matches

If screening returns a potential match against the DFAT Consolidated List, staff must hold the transaction, must not process it, and must escalate to the AML/CTF Compliance Officer on the same business day.
